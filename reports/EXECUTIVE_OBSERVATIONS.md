# Session 1 descriptive observations

The dataset supports comparison of three randomized groups over two weeks. It does not yet support a final rollout decision in this project.

- Men’s email: 267 conversions among 21,307 assigned customers (1.2531%).
- Women’s email: 189 among 21,387 (0.8837%).
- No-email control: 122 among 21,306 (0.5726%).
- Observed conversion differences versus control: +0.6805 and +0.3111 percentage points, respectively. Relative differences are larger-looking (+118.84% and +54.33%) because the control conversion rate is low; report absolute effects alongside relative effects.
- Average observed spend per assigned customer: $1.4226, $1.0772 and $0.6528 respectively. These are not profit figures.
- The preliminary allocation check is not flagged at p<0.001 (chi-square=0.20253125, df=2, p=0.903693). That does not audit assignment integrity or establish treatment effectiveness.
- Maximum absolute descriptive standardized baseline difference among the implemented pairwise comparisons is 0.01690. This summarizes measured covariates, not unobserved factors.

Practical next step: quantify uncertainty for the prespecified project outcomes and declared contrast families in Session 2, examine skewed spend, and distinguish practical importance from statistical evidence. Keep segments exploratory until interaction analysis and multiplicity controls are available. Before modern deployment, validate relevance and economics in a contemporary test.

All figures derive from reports/arm_kpis.csv, reports/comparisons.csv, reports/integrity.json and reports/baseline_balance.csv. No effects have been labeled statistically significant here.
