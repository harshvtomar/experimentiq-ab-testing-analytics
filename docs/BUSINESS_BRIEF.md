# Analytical brief
Which email treatment, if any, should a retailer consider deploying to improve purchasing and revenue?

Decision audience: product, retail and marketing managers. Unit of assignment and analysis: one source customer row. The source describes random assignment to three groups, with two-week follow-up. Keep both merchandise email treatments separate; no pooled treatment estimand is planned.

Primary outcome: purchase conversion, analyzed over all assigned customers. Secondary outcomes: website visit and spend per assigned customer. Compare each treatment with no-email control; the email-versus-email contrast is also reported descriptively. Historical channel, recency, spending bands, geography, newbie and merchandise indicators describe baseline audiences. They are not post-treatment features.

Session 1 establishes provenance, complete denominators, quality checks, independent SQL reconciliation and transparent observed comparisons. It does not select a statistically proven winner.

Before rollout: validate outcome instrumentation and assignment integrity; quantify uncertainty and practical effect size; handle multiple comparisons; assess skewed revenue and segment multiplicity; specify campaign costs, margins and decision thresholds; consider a contemporary replication. Costs and margins are absent. No profit or ROI is asserted.

Success for this session: reproducible authentic data ingestion; all 64,000 assignments retained; defensible KPI definitions; meaningful tests; usable dashboard; preserved GitHub checkpoint.
